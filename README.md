# 🏃 Aerofit Case Study

## 📌 Overview  
This repository contains the **Aerofit Treadmill Case Study**, analyzing customer segments, product positioning, and strategic recommendations.  
The study uses **segmentation by demographics, education, marital status, usage patterns, fitness levels, income, and distance run** to identify how Aerofit can position its three treadmill models (KP281, KP481, KP781) in the market.

## 💡 Business Insights  

1. **Market Segmentation**  
   - **KP281** → Entry-level treadmill for young, budget-conscious users.  
   - **KP481** → Mid-tier treadmill for balanced, moderate-income households.  
   - **KP781** → Premium treadmill for affluent, professional, and fitness-focused buyers.  

2. **Cross-Dimensional Consistency**  
   - KP281 = Young + budget + light users  
   - KP481 = Mid-income + moderate usage  
   - KP781 = Affluent + high fitness + heavy usage  

3. **Positioning Strategy**  
   - KP281 → “Affordable starter treadmill for young professionals.”  
   - KP481 → “Reliable everyday treadmill for active households.”  
   - KP781 → “Premium performance treadmill for athletes & fitness enthusiasts.”

---

## 🛍️ Business Actions  

1. **Target Younger Segments**  
   - Teens & 20s = largest base → influencer tie-ups, youth campaigns.  

2. **Leverage Education Levels**  
   - Most are well-educated → fact-driven marketing with performance metrics.  

3. **Marital Status Targeting**  
   - KP781: family bundles, couple discounts.  

4. **Usage Categories**  
   - Heavy KP781 users → loyalty programs & premium services.  
   - Light users → introductory plans to increase engagement.  

5. **Fitness Segments**  
   - KP781 = high-fitness → premium branding.  
   - KP281/KP481 = moderate fitness → everyday fitness companion.  

6. **Income Groups**  
   - KP781 → bundled with premium perks (training, accessories, support).  
   - KP281/KP481 → affordable pricing, EMI options, discounts.  

7. **Miles Run**  
   - KP781 → emphasize durability & performance.  
   - KP281/KP481 → highlight convenience & comfort.  

8. **Product-Level Actions**  
   - **KP281** = Mass product → retail promos, volume discounts.  
   - **KP481** = Mid-segment → best value-for-money positioning.  
   - **KP781** = Premium → luxury lifestyle upgrade, gym/club tie-ups.
